## What's in the full pack

- **10 looks, each in Full + Soft (50%) strength** — 20 creative LUTs, with strength control built in (no opacity guesswork on skin tones)
- **3 formats for every look:**
  - `.cube` — Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, CapCut, OBS, FFmpeg
  - **HALD PNG** — Photoshop, Affinity, Luminar, Darktable, mobile LUT apps
  - **Lightroom / Camera Raw `.xmp` presets** — Lightroom Classic 11+, Camera Raw 14+
- **Prep — Flat to Balanced utility LUT (+ Soft)** for log or flat footage, plus a 30-second log-footage workflow guide. This prevents the #1 mistake buyers make with LUTs: applying a creative look straight to LOG footage
- **13 before/after previews computed from the actual LUT files** — what you see is exactly what the file does, not hand-tuned marketing screenshots
- **Documentation:** quick-start page, per-software install guides, a recipe cheat sheet ("which look for which shot"), troubleshooting, and a plain-language commercial license

## Compatibility

**Video:** Premiere Pro · DaVinci Resolve · Final Cut Pro · CapCut · OBS · FFmpeg
**Photo:** Photoshop · Lightroom Classic / CC · Camera Raw · Affinity Photo · Luminar · Darktable · mobile LUT apps

## Requirements

- **Rec.709 footage** (phone footage, consumer cameras in standard mode, screen recordings) — apply the looks directly
- **LOG footage** (S-Log, C-Log, V-Log) — convert first; the guide and Prep LUT are included
- Lightroom presets require Lightroom Classic 11+ / Camera Raw 14+
